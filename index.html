ABOUT GRAMCONNECT

From complaint to resolution

GramConnect is a student project concept to make community concerns easier to report, follow and review. It includes village, school and public-service issues, with text, voice and photo evidence.

Our goal: To make it easier for residents, students and community members to communicate their problems.

Safety: Do not submit emergencies, allegations involving child safety, personal medical details, passwords, identity documents or other highly sensitive information through this demo. Such matters need appropriate official and confidential reporting channels.
